Hello, I'm

About Me Sarah Johnson

Writer, thinker, and mindfulness advocate sharing thoughts on life, growth, and everything in between.

My Story

I've always been fascinated by the human experience - our thoughts, emotions, and the stories we tell ourselves. After spending a decade in corporate communications, I realized my true passion lay in exploring these themes more deeply.

In 2018, I left my full-time job to pursue writing and mindfulness studies. This blog became my creative outlet, a space to share insights from my personal journey and the wisdom I've gathered from mentors, books, and life experiences.

When I'm not writing, you'll find me hiking in the mountains, practicing yoga, or curled up with a good book and a cup of herbal tea. I believe in living intentionally and finding beauty in the ordinary.

My Values

  • Authenticity

    I believe in showing up as my true self and encouraging others to do the same. No filters, no pretenses - just real stories and honest reflections.

  • Mindfulness

    Being present in each moment allows us to experience life more fully. I practice and write about mindfulness as a path to greater peace and clarity.

  • Connection

    We're all in this together. My writing aims to create bridges between people, helping us see our shared humanity despite our differences.
